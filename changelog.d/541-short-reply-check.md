- A one-word reply the voice tracker files under the wrong voice no
  longer takes that voice's name. When nothing in a turn is long enough
  to fingerprint, the app now scores the whole turn against everyone's
  saved voice, and leaves it unnamed when it plainly isn't that voice's
  person. The voice keeps its name. It adds about 40 to 50 ms to those
  short turns only, and in loud noise a right short reply can go unnamed
  now and then.

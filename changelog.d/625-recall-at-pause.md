- In a voice chat, the memory lookup now starts a third of a second
  into your pause, from the live transcript, instead of after the pause
  runs out. It's usually done by the time your turn ends, which takes
  up to half a second off each spoken reply. When you carry on talking,
  or your final words differ, it starts again as before. A lookup is
  also kept when the final transcript settles a word differently.

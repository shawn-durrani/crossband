- A new measurement, the turn-taking rig (`eval_turns`), plays made-up
  moments into the real voice page in a headless browser and times what
  it does. It counts how often a pause in the middle of a sentence ends
  your turn, and whether talking over a reply cuts it only when it
  should: an interruption, but not a "yeah", a remark to someone else or
  the TV. You can run it from the Analysis page. Nothing in the app
  changes.

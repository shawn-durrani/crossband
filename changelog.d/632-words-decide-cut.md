- Not every word you say over a spoken reply cuts it off any more. A
  "yeah", "mm-hm" or "right" lets the reply carry on and isn't sent to
  the AIs. A remark that starts with the name of someone the app knows,
  like "Sam, pass the salt", lets it carry on too and is sent as usual.
  "Stop", "wait", "hang on" or an AI's name cut at once, and so does an
  answer to something the AI just asked. Otherwise it takes two words of
  your own.

- A voice turn is sent once after the app locks during a call. A restart
  signs the browser out, and when the lock screen took over, the call kept
  running out of sight with its microphone open. Once you unlocked and
  started voice again, two sessions heard you, so every turn reached the
  chat twice and each seat answered it twice. The call now ends when the
  lock screen appears, and starting voice ends any older session on the
  page first.

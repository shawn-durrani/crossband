  that stopped with an error says so and shows the error, and one
  someone stopped says it was stopped, instead of both reading as
  finished. While the work runs, the AIs and the strip over the
  composer count the parts made so far, so nobody says nothing's on
  screen when it is. And when a message you sent mid-build answered the
  app's plan or question, the AIs hear that it's no longer waiting.
